Join the readers

No editor approves what Speculum publishes. The record is checked by fixed rules, by an independent source check on every item of research, and by its readers. Readers see new work before it reaches this site, and tell us what is wrong, unclear or missing.

What readers do

  • Read new work early. At early.speculum.vote: editions of The Tide, research in The Dependents still being checked, and sections still being built, before they are published here.
  • Say what is wrong. Reply to any email from us, or use the Dispute link beside an item in The Dependents. A dispute needs a source, and it withdraws the item until that source has been read beside the original.
  • Check the work, not the verdict. Readers improve how the record is kept. They do not decide what it finds: every figure still comes from the filings by the published rules, the same for every member and party.

How it works

Leave your email below. Each request is read by a person. When yours is approved, you'll get an email explaining how to sign in. There is no password: you enter your email at early.speculum.vote and receive a one-time code.
