Method

Speculum describes; it does not accuse. Every reading below is a rate or a dollar figure computed the same way for every member, compared with the chamber and with the member's own party.

Readings in this version

ReadingHow it is computedSource
Small donorsUnitemized individual contributions (donors giving $200 or less in the cycle) as a share of all contributions: individuals, committees, party, and the candidate.FEC candidate totals
PACs and committeesContributions from PACs and other non-party committees as a share of the same total.FEC candidate totals
Lobbyist moneySum of contributions listed on LD-203 reports whose honoree is this member, matched by name.lda.gov
Party unityOn votes where most Democrats opposed most Republicans, the share of the member's yes-or-no votes cast with their party's majority. Independents count with the party they caucus with.Roll calls
VotedRoll calls the member voted on, as a share of roll calls held while they served.Roll calls

Campaign cycles

House figures cover the current two-year cycle. Senate figures cover the senator's full six-year election cycle, because senators raise money across their whole term.

Known limits

  • Lobbyist contributions are matched to members by the honoree name written on each report. Names vary, so some matches will be missed and a few may be wrong. Every amount links to its filing.
  • A high share of PAC money or a high party-unity rate is not evidence of wrongdoing. They describe how a member is funded and how they vote.
  • Readings that need coded funder positions (Independence, Exposure, Timing) are not shown until those positions have been reviewed by people.

Archive

Each night every raw file fetched from a source is saved untouched, hashed with SHA-256, and listed in a manifest attached to that night's release, so any figure can be traced to the exact bytes it came from.
