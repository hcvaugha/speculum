A chain judges a decision, never a person. It names who voted and who was funded, and lets the record of what followed speak. Every link carries an evidence grade and a source, outcomes a decision prevented are recorded as faithfully as outcomes it caused, and a member who voted to block a sale is not tied to what the sale did. Research is done with AI. An item appears on the public site only after an independent AI checker has opened its source and confirmed that it says what the item says; anyone can dispute an item with a source, and it is withdrawn until checked again.
